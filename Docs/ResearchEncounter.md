# R-09 containment encounter — 0.14.2

The mouth revision replaces the inflated maxilla and oval chin with a tapered bony rostrum and an open, flattened mandibular arch. Thin labial borders continue into the cheeks, with recessed alveolar ridges and varied rooted teeth. A concave, opaque buccal membrane leads to a narrow throat; a grooved tongue sits behind the lower teeth. The lining blends between Head and Jaw while the lower arch, teeth and tongue follow the hinged mandible. Attack recovery closes the jaw continuously. These surfaces reuse the existing skin and oral PBR maps. The reproducible geometry is in `Tools/Blender/aquatic_mouth.py`; URP captures 124-126 show the front, profile and attack gape.

The research archive now has 28 textured fluid-preservation jars across four shelves: paired eyes, curled fictional embryos, animal heads and antlered anatomical variants. The head specimens adapt the retained CC0 NewDLC wolf anatomy with its original UVs, licensed fur/bone maps and project-authored preserved tissue. The antlered variant is fictional, not a scientifically accurate deer model. Glass, lids, labels, preservative and biological surfaces all use mapped materials.

R-09 is an original 20-bone aquatic humanoid. The revised anatomy uses a fused torso and musculature, recessed eye sockets and eyelids, a recessed mouth, irregular teeth, curved digits and recessed gill slits and a thin dorsal membrane. The large abdominal plates and cylindrical gill shapes have been removed. The six skeletal clips are Idle, Escape (4 seconds), Run, Attack, Hit and Death. The stride now solves planted ankle positions and its phase follows actual distance travelled. Acceleration, direction-facing and crossfades are smoothed; path corners consume the remaining movement distance in the same frame. AI owns world movement; clips do not move the player or teleport the creature through walls. The escape plays in the world, so the player can watch it or retreat.

The E control beside the tank starts five active seconds of red siren and draining. Menus pause the sequence and sound. At five seconds the alarm stops, the front glass panels open over 1.2 seconds, attached life-support hoses drop away and the four-second emergence animation begins. Pursuit then uses the room/forest navigation mesh, world collision and shelter boundaries.

R-09 takes exactly four successful shotgun hits, axe strikes or a mixture. Misses and blocked lines of sight do not count. Its committed attack can miss a retreating player. Two landed attacks separated by less than five active seconds kill the player; after the window expires a fresh pair is required. This recovery timer is separate from existing fire and physical wounds.

The gate key hangs visibly from a chain of interlocking links around the actual neck bone. It follows the creature and its death pose. After death the key softly flashes gold at 1.25Hz; E takes it from the settled corpse, then removes the key and stops its glow while leaving the chain. Pause freezes the pulse; saves retain the key/kill state. Life-support meshes belong to a separate tank-owned group, drop during release and are all hidden before pursuit. The fuse powers the circuit. A separate key lock beside the starting cabin's fuse panel must then be turned with E; its cylinder rotates and the gate slides open. The exit checks both requirements. English and Hebrew prompts, objectives, hints and the research note describe the neck chain.

The new `LabAbyssDermisV2` colour texture was made with built-in imagegen and is retained unchanged alongside the original output. Fine pore normals, nonmetallic roughness/AO and bounded dermal thickness are technical derivatives. The existing skin shader supplies restrained dielectric highlights and light diffusion; UV mapping holds the skin on the animated mesh. Exact prompt, source paths, hash and output dimensions are in `ArtSource/Research/Materials/DermisV2Imagegen.json`. Authoring sources are `Tools/Blender/aquatic_anatomy.py`, `research_creature.py` and `Tools/Assets/prepare_aquatic_dermis.py`.

Save schema 6 persists release phase/time, creature health/position, the five-second injury window, key recovery and the turned lock. New Game restores the sealed tank. Version 1–5 saves migrate without losing resources or completed progress; an unfinished powered gate now requires the research key. Already completed legacy games remain complete.
